CREATE WITH AI

From an idea
to an arrangement.

Ask for a melody, beat or full arrangement. Set an optional creative brief, or rewrite one selected clip while preserving the rest of your session.

  1. Connect. Open Create with AI and sign in with ChatGPT through Codex, or connect your OpenAI API key.
  2. Choose. Select GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Luna, or GPT-6.1 Sol and a supported reasoning effort.
  3. Create. Describe your music and optionally set style, key, bars, energy and development. Refine composition adds one review pass; switch it off for faster requests. Listen to the local preview, inspect the musical review, then apply the proposal as one undoable edit.

AI generation needs internet access and an eligible OpenAI account. Codex uses your account limits; API usage is billed separately. Prompts, MIDI patterns, settings and enabled local music measurements are sent to OpenAI. Recordings and rendered previews stay on your computer.

Prefer your browser?

Sonora works on macOS, Linux and Windows in a modern browser. The Codex connection is available in the Windows desktop app.
