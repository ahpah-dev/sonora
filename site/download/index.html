CREATE WITH AI

From an idea
to an arrangement.

Ask for a melody, a beat, a full arrangement, or a change to your current session. Sonora turns the response into editable MIDI and instrument settings.

  1. Connect. Open Create with AI and sign in with ChatGPT through Codex, or connect your OpenAI API key.
  2. Choose. Select GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Luna, or GPT-6.1 Sol and a supported reasoning effort.
  3. Create. Describe your music, review the proposed changes, then apply them. Every proposal can be undone.

AI generation needs internet access and an eligible OpenAI account. Codex uses your account limits; API usage is billed separately. Prompts, MIDI patterns and settings are sent to OpenAI. Imported recordings are not uploaded.

Prefer your browser?

Sonora works on macOS, Linux and Windows in a modern browser. The Codex connection is available in the Windows desktop app.
