COMMON QUESTIONS

Getting started.
No installation required.

Is Sonora free and open source?

Yes. Sonora is free to use and released under the MIT License. You can inspect the source, modify it, and contribute on GitHub.

Which input devices are supported?

Your computer keyboard and the on-screen keys are ready to play. In Chrome or Edge, you can also connect a MIDI keyboard and use its sustain pedal.

Can I customize the instruments?

Yes. Customize the piano’s brightness, hammer, strings, decay and release. Explore the synth designer for layered oscillators, filters and modulation, then save your own patches.

How are projects saved?

Projects stay in this browser. Download a Sonora project to keep a portable backup, or export your music as WAV or MIDI to share it.
