Do I need a keyboard?

Your computer keyboard and the on-screen keys are ready to play. In Chrome or Edge, you can also connect a MIDI keyboard and use its sustain pedal.

Can I make these sounds my own?

Yes. Customize the synthesized piano’s brightness, hammer, strings, decay and release. Explore the synth designer for layered oscillators, filters and modulation, then save your own patches.

Where does my music go?

Projects stay in this browser. Download a Sonora project to keep a portable backup, or export your music as WAV or MIDI to share it.
